# FacultyHack@Gateways 2026 Curriculum Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on redesigning **Human Anatomy & Physiology I Laboratory (SBIO 221LB)** at **Southern University and A&M College** by integrating accessible Science Gateway, computational, and AI-supported tools into laboratory instruction. The primary goal is to improve students' understanding of complex anatomical and physiological concepts through **interactive visualization, data analysis, and hands-on computational learning**.

The redesigned curriculum incorporates tools such as **BioDigital Human** and **Jupyter Notebook/JupyterLab** to help students visualize anatomical structures, analyze physiological data, interpret results, and connect laboratory concepts to real-world clinical and health-care applications. The project also introduces students to computational thinking, data literacy, and the responsible use of AI in science.

## Science Gateway Resources & Technology Notes

The redesigned Anatomy & Physiology laboratory curriculum incorporates accessible visualization, computational, data-analysis, and AI-supported resources. These tools are intended to expand students' access to interactive learning experiences while developing their ability to interpret biological data and apply anatomical and physiological concepts.

### Tools Used

**BioDigital Human**
* **Description:** BioDigital Human is an interactive 3D visualization platform that allows users to explore human anatomy, physiology, diseases, and treatments using detailed digital models.
* **Course Use:** Students will use BioDigital Human to explore anatomical structures and body systems in three dimensions. Activities will help students identify structures, examine spatial relationships, connect anatomy to physiology, and apply concepts to clinical scenarios.

**Jupyter Notebook / JupyterLab**
* **Description:** Jupyter provides an interactive computational environment where students can organize, analyze, visualize, and interpret scientific data.
* **Course Use:** Students will use guided Jupyter activities to work with physiological datasets, including **EMG and EEG data**. Students will organize data, generate visualizations, identify patterns, and interpret results without requiring extensive prior programming experience.

**AI-Supported Learning Tools**
* **Description:** AI tools can assist students with explanations, data interpretation, scientific reasoning, and the evaluation of scientific information when used responsibly.
* **Course Use:** Students will participate in guided activities that require them to evaluate and fact-check AI-generated explanations using course materials and scientific evidence. These activities will emphasize responsible AI use rather than simply accepting AI-generated answers.

**Canvas LMS and McGraw Hill Connect**
* **Description:** Canvas and McGraw Hill Connect provide digital environments for distributing course materials, assignments, assessments, and learning resources.
* **Course Use:** These platforms will support delivery of Science Gateway activities, laboratory assignments, instructions, assessments, and supporting resources.

### Implementation Notes

* **Session 1:** Reviewed the existing Anatomy & Physiology laboratory curriculum and identified opportunities to integrate Science Gateway and computational resources. Established the project goal of improving visualization, data analysis, and interactive learning.
* **Session 2:** Explored **BioDigital Human** as an accessible tool for interactive 3D anatomy instruction. Considered activities that allow students to visualize anatomical structures, examine relationships among body systems, and connect structure to function.
* **Session 3:** Explored **Jupyter Notebook/JupyterLab** for guided physiological data-analysis activities. Identified opportunities for students to organize, graph, and interpret EMG and EEG data while developing introductory computational and data-literacy skills.
* **Curriculum Integration:** Revised course activities and learning objectives to strengthen progression through **Bloom's Taxonomy**, from remembering and understanding anatomical concepts to applying, analyzing, evaluating, and creating.
* **Responsible AI Integration:** Developed opportunities for students to critically evaluate AI-generated biological information and compare responses with reliable scientific and course resources.

### Science Gateway Goals

The primary Science Gateway goal for this project is to:

> **Integrate accessible Science Gateway, computational, and AI tools such as BioDigital Human and Jupyter into the Anatomy & Physiology laboratory to improve students' understanding of complex biological concepts through visualization, data analysis, and interactive learning.**

Additional project goals include:

* Integrate Science Gateway resources into teaching and learning activities.
* Expand student access to computing tools, scientific data, and hands-on learning.
* Strengthen students' data-literacy and computational-thinking skills.
* Connect Anatomy & Physiology concepts to clinical and health-care applications.
* Promote responsible and evidence-based use of AI.
* Provide faculty and students with appropriate training and technical support.

### Resource Needs / Questions

**1. How can Science Gateways support our courses and research?**

Science Gateways can provide accessible platforms for visualization, computational analysis, scientific datasets, and interactive learning. In Anatomy & Physiology, these resources can allow students to move beyond memorization by exploring three-dimensional anatomy, analyzing physiological data, interpreting results, and applying biological concepts to real-world situations.

**2. What training and computing resources are available?**

Resources of particular interest include faculty development, student onboarding materials, starter Jupyter notebooks, A&P datasets, quick-start guides, technical support, access and licensing guidance, and mentoring to support sustainable implementation of Science Gateway resources beyond the initial pilot.
